**Anchor Point:** Grace Cathedral (1100 California St, San Francisco, CA 94108)  
**Total Mapped Destinations:** 43 campgrounds across 8 distinct geographic sectors

## ⚡ The SF Camper Reservation Cheat Sheet

| Agency / System | Rolling Window | Release Time | Key Destinations Covered | Tactical Advice |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **California State Parks** (*ReserveCalifornia*) | **6 Months Rolling** | **8:00:00 AM PT** | Steep Ravine, Mt. Tamalpais, Big Sur (Pfeiffer), Salt Point, Big Basin, Half Moon Bay | Set digital atomic clock; site availability opens *exactly* at 08:00:00. Have profile logged in and card on file. Sells out in < 5 seconds for weekends. |
| **National Park Service (NPS)** (*Recreation.gov*) | **3 to 6 Months Rolling** | **7:00:00 AM PT** | Point Reyes Backcountry (3 mo), Kirby Cove (3 mo), Pinnacles (6 mo), Lassen (6 mo), Whiskeytown (6 mo) | Kirby Cove and Wildcat/Coast camps drop on 3-month rolling basis. Pinnacles & Lassen drop on 6-month rolling. |
| **Yosemite National Park** (*Recreation.gov*) | **1 Month Blocks (5 mos ahead)** | **7:00:00 AM PT (15th of month)** | Upper Pines, Lower Pines, North Pines | Released on the 15th of every month at 7 AM for a 1-month booking block 4-5 months out. Camp 4 uses 1-day daily lottery. |
| **US Forest Service (USFS)** (*Recreation.gov*) | **6 Months Rolling** | **7:00:00 AM PT** | Kirk Creek (Big Sur), Plaskett Creek, Shasta-Trinity NF | Kirk Creek ocean bluff sites are premier. Several sites kept for walk-in FCFS. |
| **East Bay Regional Parks (EBRPD)** | **12 Weeks (84 days)** | **Midnight / Portal** | Anthony Chabot, Del Valle, Sunol | Much more accessible for spontaneous weekend getaways; dog-friendly. |
| **San Mateo County Parks** | **180 Days (6 Months)** | **Midnight** | Memorial Park, Sam McDonald | Under-the-radar redwood camping; great family sites along Pescadero Creek. |
| **First-Come, First-Served (FCFS)** | **Same-Day Arrival** | **Morning of (7-9 AM)** | Pantoll & Bootjack (Mt. Tam), Prewitt Ridge, Trinity Alps Trailheads | Arrive Thursday evening or by 8:00 AM Friday. Mt. Tamalpais kiosks accept card/cash. |
| **BLM / Dispersed Wilderness** | **No Reservation ($0)** | **Anytime** | Prewitt Ridge (Big Sur), Trinity Alps Wilderness, Panoche Hills | Requires California Campfire Permit (free online test). Leave No Trace; pack in all water. |

### 📍 Sector: San Francisco
*Total Campgrounds in Sector:* 1

#### **Rob Hill Campground**
- **Managing Agency:** Presidio Trust / NPS
- **Drive from Grace Cathedral:** **12 mins** (~4.3 road miles | 3.3 mi straight-line)
- **Reservation Type:** `Paid Reservation`
- **When Reservations Open:** Opens in February for entire season (Apr-Oct) at 9:00 AM PT via Recreation.gov
- **Capacity & Site Types:** 4 sites (Group / Tent camping (up to 30 people each)) | **Fee:** $135 - $175 / group site

- **Overview & Insider Tips:** The only public campground in San Francisco! Perched in a cypress grove overlooking Baker Beach and Pacific ocean.
