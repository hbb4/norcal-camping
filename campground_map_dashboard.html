⚡ Booking Window Cheat Sheet

  • ReserveCalifornia: 6 months rolling at 8:00:00 AM PT. Steep Ravine, Big Sur, Sugar Pine Point open daily.
  • Recreation.gov: Most sites 6 months rolling at 7:00:00 AM PT. Desolation Wilderness permits & Eastern Sierra campgrounds open 6 months rolling. Yosemite opens 15th of month.
  • First-Come, First-Served: Mt. Tamalpais (Pantoll/Bootjack) is open daily year-round; midweek is easy, but weekend campers should arrive by Friday 8:00 AM.
