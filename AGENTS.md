# AGENTS.md - Camping Project Workspace

This workspace is dedicated to researching, mapping, and planning camping trips across Northern and Central California, anchored from San Francisco (Grace Cathedral).

## Priorities & Workflows
1. **Campground Data Management**: Maintain structured datasets (`campgrounds_data.json`) covering NPS, CA State Parks, USFS, BLM, and Regional Parks.
2. **Visual Mapping & Dashboards**: Maintain interactive visual tools and static cartographic plots covering the Greater Bay Area and regional extensions (North Coast, Shasta-Cascade/Trinity, Sonoma/Napa, Monterey Bay/Big Sur, Sierra).
3. **Reservation Intelligence**: Track reservation release schedules, cancelation hunting strategies, FCFS arrival tactics, and seasonal weather/access advisories.
